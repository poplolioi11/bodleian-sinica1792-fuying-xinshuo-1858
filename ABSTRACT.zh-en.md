# 婦嬰新說 / Fu ying xin shuo

《婦嬰新說》（Fu ying xin shuo）由合信氏（Benjamin Hobson）著、管茂材同撰，館目著錄為1858年上海仁濟醫館本，論述女性生殖解剖、妊娠、分娩、嬰幼兒疾病及當時方藥，並附醫學圖說。本資料集依牛津Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford所藏Sinica 1792，提供128面忠實轉錄，以及p129–137九面的版本與流通史導讀；導讀不收該章操作正文。JSON、TXT及TEI保留原字、原刻句讀、細註、圖中文字及來源對應，可供醫學史、翻譯史與圖說研究初步檢索及回圖查核。v0.1.0為AI影像初錄及同底本補錄，尚未套用獨立二校，保留12條有限未解紀錄，未經人類全文核對。

Fu ying xin shuo (婦嬰新說), written by Benjamin Hobson with Guan mao cai and catalogued as an 1858 Shanghai edition issued by Ren ji yi guan, discusses female reproductive anatomy, pregnancy, childbirth, childhood illness and historical remedies, with medical illustrations. This dataset draws on Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford, Sinica 1792. It provides a source-faithful transcription of 128 faces and a bibliographical and circulation-history guide for the nine faces p129–137, without reproducing that chapter’s operational text. JSON, TXT and TEI preserve source characters, printed punctuation, small notes and diagram labels, with links to the witness, for preliminary research in medical history, translation and illustration. Version 0.1.0 is an AI image transcription with same-witness supplementation; independent collation has not been applied. Twelve bounded uncertainty records remain, and no human full-text collation is claimed.

館目原拼音：`fu ying xin shuo`。展示名僅大寫首字。英文意譯「New Treatise on Women and Infants」由編者提供，不是館方正式英題。
