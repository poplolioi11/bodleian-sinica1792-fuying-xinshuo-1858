# 方法與限制 / Methods and limitations

依牛津 Bodleian Libraries 所藏 Sinica 1792 的影像逐面轉錄，保留原字、異體、原有圈點與疑讀，不另加現代標點或依文義改字。既有128面、1504個文字單元保留先前獨立 AI 讀稿比較及知悉初稿後對照來源的覆核狀態；新增9面、103個文字單元為 AI 輔助初錄，另對實際取得的局部細圖回看，沒有把137面視為全文獨立二校。

JSON、TXT 與 TEI 依同一固定文字匯出，逐字核對讀序與來源對應。小註另存與正文的關係；衍生閱讀檔以編輯標記顯示兩處新增小註的插入位置，不改動原文層。共保留51筆當前疑讀。格式檢查採 TEI P5 4.12.0 與 CFF 1.2.0 正式規格；這些檢查不代表文字準確率。尚未經人類全文校勘、專家審定或同儕審查。實際 AI 模型與完整用量資料未能確認。原圖、裁片、專用程式、提示與完整工作紀錄未隨資料公開，因此不宣稱可由公開包完整重跑影像辨讀流程。

The transcription follows the images of Bodleian Libraries, University of Oxford, Sinica 1792, face by face, retaining source characters, variants, printed punctuation and uncertain readings without modernization or contextual correction. The existing 128 faces and 1504 text units retain their previous independent AI reading comparison and subsequent source review conducted with access to the draft. The nine added faces and 103 units are AI-assisted initial transcription, with limited checks against the detail images actually obtained. This does not constitute independent collation of all 137 faces.

JSON, TXT and TEI are generated from the same fixed transcription and checked for exact text order and source correspondence. Notes retain separate links to the body text; a reading derivative uses explicit editorial markers for the two newly positioned notes without changing the literal layer. There are 51 current uncertainty records. Formal checks use TEI P5 4.12.0 and CFF 1.2.0; these checks do not measure textual accuracy. Human full-text collation, expert review and peer review have not been performed. Exact AI model identity and complete usage data are unconfirmed. Source images, crops, specialized programs, prompts and full working records are not distributed, so the image-reading process cannot be fully reproduced from the public package.
